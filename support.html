Playing

How do I build a wall?

Drag a finger across the sand between the sea (at the top) and your towers. The drag digs a trench and piles the sand up in a ridge on each side.

What does packing do?

Rest a finger still on a ridge for about half a second. A ring fills under it and the sand darkens. Packed sand washes away about three times more slowly than loose sand.

What is the white line on the water?

The next wave, a few seconds out. A thicker line is a bigger wave, and its slant shows which side of the beach it reaches first. Every seventh wave is much bigger, with a thick white crest.

Why did my tower fall?

Water stood at its foot for too long. A tower cracks, then wobbles, then crumbles; if it dries out in time it recovers. After a fall, a short replay shows the water's route and circles the gap it came through.

How is the score worked out?

Only from what you do: waves turned away (several in a row build a combo), seventh waves held without damage, the towel and the bucket kept dry, shells picked up, and towers still standing at high tide. Nothing is scored for time passing.

What are the shells for?

A wave can leave a shell or a starfish on the wet sand as it pulls back. Tap it before the next wave takes it for bonus points.

Beaches and modes

What is Today's Beach?

One beach each day, the same for everyone, changing at midnight UTC. Your first finished run of the day is your score; later runs are practice.

How do I unlock Spring Tide?

Finish one run on a beach to unlock its Spring Tide, which has a higher tide and bigger waves.

What is Free Dig?

The beach with no clock and no score, for trying things out. You can turn the waves off.

Settings and accessibility

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes: Settings in the game has switches for both, and separate volumes for the surf and the effects.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. The board describes the towers, the next wave and the nearest water, and each control is labelled. The game also follows Reduce Motion and Differentiate Without Colour.

Do I need Game Center?

No. The game plays the same whether or not you are signed in, and it keeps your scores on the device.

Price

Is it free? Are there ads or in-app purchases?

It is free, with every beach and mode open. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings in the game > Reset progress. This clears best scores, daily attempts and unlocks on this device; Game Center scores stay with Game Center.
