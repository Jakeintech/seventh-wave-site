In one line

Dig, pack and hold the line. A sandcastle, a rising tide, and every seventh wave is the big one.

Description

Build a sandcastle. Then keep it standing while the tide comes in.

Drag your finger to dig a trench and throw up a wall. Hold still to pack the sand hard. Every grain you move comes from somewhere, so every wall leaves a moat. The sea is simulated: water runs downhill, finds the low spot and spills through it.

Waves arrive in sets, and a line on the horizon shows the size and angle of the next one. Every seventh wave is the big one. Turn waves away to build a combo, keep the towel and the bucket dry, pick up shells the sea leaves behind, and reach high tide with your three towers still standing.

When a tower falls, a short replay shows the path the water took and the gap it found. One tap and you are back on the same beach.

What is different about it

  • The sea is a simulation, not an animation: water runs downhill, finds the lowest point of a wall and spills through it.
  • Sand is conserved. Every wall is dug from somewhere, so every wall leaves a moat.
  • The score moves only when the player does something: there are no points for time passing.
  • When a tower falls, a two-second replay shows the water's route and circles the gap.
  • Today's Beach is the same for every player each day.
