Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version.

  • Seventh Wave collects no data. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • Your scores, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game has no server of its own. If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, scores and achievements go to Game Center.

1. Who we are

Seventh Wave is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

2. What the game handles

3. What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or notifications.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and it contains no advertising.
  • It makes no network requests of its own.

4. Children

Seventh Wave is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no links out of the game except to this site, and no purchases.

5. Your choices

  • Reset your progress at any time in the game: Settings > Reset progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
  • Game Center is optional. Sign out in the Settings app > Game Center and the game plays the same.

6. This website

This site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, as the host, receives the technical information any web server does (such as your IP address) under its own privacy statement.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the game ever started collecting data, the App Store privacy label would change first.
